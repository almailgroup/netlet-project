# The sign-in page's picture

`welcome.webp` here is the picture beside the sign-in and create-account forms
on a wide screen, and the band across their top on a tablet or a phone:
NetLet's own street — four stores and the van being loaded, the towers behind.
Replace it by dropping in another file named `welcome.webp` (or `welcome.png` /
`welcome.jpg`); remove it and the card is the form alone.

Landscape 4:3, about 1120 × 840 and under 100 KB, with edges in the brand navy
`#0a285a`. It is drawn cropped to fill — tall beside the form on a wide screen,
a short band on a phone — so keep what matters in the middle: the stores and
the van. Decorative — the form's title says what the page is — so it carries
no alt text. `phone/welcome.webp` beside it is its phone copy, about half the
bytes.
